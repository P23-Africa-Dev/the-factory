1. Go to Workforce > Agents and add a team member.
2. Only the full name, email and role are required. Phone, zones, work days, salary and supervisor are optional.
3. The person receives an email invitation to set up their account.

Rules:
- Owners and admins can add admins and supervisors. Supervisors can add agents.
- A new agent can be placed under a supervisor. Owners and admins can also supervise agents.

Managing the team:
- From Workforce > Agents you can edit a member, change their role or zones, move an agent to another supervisor, resend an invitation, suspend or reactivate them, or remove them.
- Supervisors can only act on their own agents, and only if the company settings allow it.
- Territory zones (country, state, LGA) are managed from the same area.

ELY can do these too: "Move Ada under Sam", "Suspend Bola for 3 days", "Resend Tunde's invitation", "Create a zone for London, Greater London". Changes to people always need your confirmation.

ELY can do this for you: "Invite Tunde Bello, tunde@example.com, as an agent under Sam". ELY always asks you to confirm before sending an invitation.

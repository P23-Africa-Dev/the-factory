1. Connect your mailbox first (Google, Microsoft or Zoho) from your settings. Emails are sent from your own account.
2. Open the lead page and compose an email, or reply in an existing thread.
3. Sent and received emails appear on the lead timeline.

Agents can email leads they created or are assigned to.

ELY can do this for you: "Draft a follow-up email to Acme about the samples" and, after you review it, send it. ELY always asks you to confirm before an email is sent.

- Drive holds company documents in folders. Open it from your profile menu.
- Managers upload files, create folders and share files with specific people.
- You only see files shared with you.

ELY can find files and answer questions about their contents: "Find the price list", "What does the onboarding guide say about uniforms?". Managers can also ask ELY to share a file with people or delete it (after confirming). ELY cannot upload files.

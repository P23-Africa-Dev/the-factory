- Notifications appear under the bell icon. Change which ones you receive in Settings.
- Managers can send a notification to specific people or to a whole role. Supervisors can notify only their own agents.
- Meetings can be scheduled with team members and outside guests, with reminders. Connecting a calendar in Settings syncs them.

ELY can do this for you: "Remind my agents to submit visit photos by 5pm", "Schedule a meeting with Ada tomorrow at 2pm for 30 minutes". ELY asks you to confirm before sending notifications, inviting outside guests or cancelling a meeting.

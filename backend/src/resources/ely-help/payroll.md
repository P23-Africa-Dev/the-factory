- Payroll lists each agent's pay for the period, based on their salary type, attendance and commission.
- Managers review an agent's payroll and approve it, or revoke an approval, from the payroll list.
- Monthly attendance summaries for payroll are generated automatically at the start of each month.

ELY can do this for you: "Approve Ada's payroll for this month". Approving or revoking payroll always needs your confirmation.

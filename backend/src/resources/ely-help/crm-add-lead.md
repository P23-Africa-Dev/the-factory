1. Open CRM from the top menu.
2. Use the add-lead button and fill in the lead name. Phone, email, location, industry, contact person, priority and a first note are optional.
3. Pick the pipeline and stage if you do not want the defaults, then save.

Notes:
- New leads go into the default pipeline and its first stage unless you choose others.
- Leads that agents add are assigned to themselves. Managers can choose the owner.
- If a lead with the same details already exists you may be offered a merge instead.

ELY can do this for you: "Add a lead called Bright Pharmacy, phone +447911123456, in Shoreditch".

- A pipeline is a sales process. Its stages (labels) are the columns on the CRM board.
- Switch pipelines from the pipeline selector at the top of CRM. "All Pipelines" shows every pipeline.
- Owners, admins and supervisors can create pipelines and manage stages with "Manage Labels": add, rename, recolor, reorder or delete a stage, and mark a stage as won.
- Agents can view stages but cannot change them.

ELY can do this for you: "Create a pipeline called Distributors", "Add a stage called Sample Sent", "What stages do we have?".

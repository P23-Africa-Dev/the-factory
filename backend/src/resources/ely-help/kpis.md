- Managers create KPIs with a name, category, objective, target value, expected outcome, dates and priority, and assign them to team members.
- KPIs move through Pending, In progress, Completed and Cancelled. Managers can move any KPI; agents update the ones assigned to them.
- Team performance against KPIs is summarised for managers.

ELY can do this for you: "Create a KPI for 50 retailer visits this month and assign it to John", "Change the target of Retail Visits to 60", "How is the team performing?".

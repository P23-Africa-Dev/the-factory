- ELY answers questions about your organization's data (leads, tasks, projects, attendance, tracking, KPIs, drive) within what your role allows.
- ELY can do many things for you: add and update leads, notes and visits; create and update tasks, KPIs, projects and meetings; manage CRM stages and pipelines; send notifications and emails; and invite team members.
- Simple changes run straight away. Anything that sends messages, invites people, deletes records or reassigns work shows a confirmation card first. Press Confirm or Cancel.
- ELY never sees other organizations' data and cannot change billing, connected accounts or security settings; it points you to the right page instead.
- You can write to ELY in your own language.

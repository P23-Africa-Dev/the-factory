- Agents clock in at the start of the day and clock out at the end (Attendance in the agent menu). Location may be required.
- Managers see who is present, late or absent under Workforce > Attendance. Supervisors see their own team.
- Companies can set working hours; open records can be closed automatically at closing time.

ELY can tell you: "Who was late today?", "How many hours did Ada work yesterday?". Owners and admins can ask ELY to change working hours ("Set working hours to 8:00 to 17:00, Monday to Friday"), which needs confirmation. ELY cannot clock you in or out.

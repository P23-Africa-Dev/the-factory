1. Open CRM and click the lead to open its page.
2. Change the fields you need and save, or drag the lead card to another stage column on the board.
3. Use the notes area on the lead page to add a note. Visits and calls appear in the lead activity.

Rules:
- Managers (owners, admins, supervisors) can edit every field and change the owner.
- Agents can change the stage and pipeline of leads they work on, and contact details only on leads they created.
- Only owners, admins and supervisors can delete leads, one at a time or several selected leads at once.

ELY can do this for you: "Move Acme Retail to Negotiation", "Make Bright Pharmacy high priority", "Add a note to this lead: owner wants a quote next week", "Log my visit to Acme: met the buyer, they want samples".

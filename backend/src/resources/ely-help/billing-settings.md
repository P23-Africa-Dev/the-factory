- Settings holds your profile, notification preferences, connected email and calendar accounts, and company settings.
- Billing (plan, seats, payment methods, invoices) is managed by owners and admins under Settings.
- For security, ELY does not change billing, payment methods, connected accounts or security settings. Open Settings to do these yourself.
